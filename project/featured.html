Featured Review: Spirited Away

Director
Hayao Miyazaki
Studio
Studio Ghibli
Runtime
2 hr 5 min
Rated
PG
My Score
5/5

The Story

Ten-year-old Chihiro is moving to a new town when her family wanders into an abandoned theme park. By nightfall it fills with all sorts of spirits, her parents have been turned into pigs, and she has to take a job at a bathhouse run by a witch to set them free.

Art and Music

Every second is meticulously hand drawn, a beautiful artform that is almost extinct today. The world feels like a real place with its own rules, and some sections are the calmest, most beautiful scenes in any animated film. Joe Hisaishi's masterful score is unforgettable.

A coming-of-age story that trusts kids to handle something strange and a little scary.

Additional Considerations

Pros

  • Gorgeous hand-drawn animation
  • Memorable, creative characters
  • Great for kids and adults

Cons

  • Some creatures may scare young kids
  • The long runtime is a commitment to watch, more so than most animated movies.

The Verdict

If you have never seen a Studio Ghibli movie, start here. You will not regret it. Full cast and details are on TMDb.
